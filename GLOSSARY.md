# Glossary

Words we use for DepoAudio, and the words we avoid.

**DepoAudio**: the free desktop app for converting, improving, reviewing and organizing court recordings. A brand of its own, not shown as part of DepoStack.
_Avoid_: DepoStack Audio, Audio by DepoStack

**Recording**: one audio file or court-format set (SGMCA, FTR `.trm`, CourtSmart BWF) the person opens.
_Avoid_: job, upload

**Convert**: turn a recording into another format (WAV, MP3, FLAC, Opus, M4A, SGMCA Direct Audio), on the device.
_Avoid_: export (on its own), process

**Improve**: the local, non-learned level and cleanup controls (channel leveling, clipped peaks, high-pass, normalize, silence trim, fades).
_Avoid_: enhance, AI cleanup

**Case library**: where converted recordings are auto-filed by case on the device.
_Avoid_: cloud library, account library

**On the device**: work that happens on the person's computer with nothing uploaded. Today that is everything DepoAudio does with recordings.
_Avoid_: offline (update checks may still use the network)

**Send** (later, not built): a future, signed-in step that uploads one recording to Transcript or Video only when the person presses it. Waits until DepoStack is closer to production.
