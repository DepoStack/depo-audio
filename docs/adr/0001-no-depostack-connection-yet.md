# 1. No DepoStack connection yet

Date: 2026-11-24. Status: accepted (owner).

## Context
The other products are moving to depostack.com accounts and link to each other (Exhibits, Transcript, Video).
DepoAudio already has public users, its own site and a promise of no account and no uploads. An optional
sign-in that sends recordings to Transcript or Video was considered.

## Decision
DepoAudio stays a separate, free brand with no DepoStack connection until DepoStack is closer to production and
stable. No sign-in, no sending, no "by DepoStack" line in the app; the website keeps one footer credit.

## Consequences
- The no-account and no-upload promises in the README and on depoaudio.com stay true.
- People who want a recording in Transcript or Video move the file themselves for now.
- When the connection is revisited, the starting plan is optional sign-in and upload only on Send, and the
  sign-in's name has to be settled against the separate-brand decision.
